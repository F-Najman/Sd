\begin{abstract}
Let $S(d)$ be the set of primes $p$ for which there exists an elliptic curve over a number field of degree $d$ with a point of order $p$. The sets $S(d)$ are known for $d\leq 8$. We determine $S(d)$ for all $9\leq d\leq 22$. For example, the largest prime in $S(22)$ is $67$, and $S(16)$ consists of the primes up to $37$. To reduce the problem to finitely many primes we prove a version of Oesterl\'e's bound which uses reduction modulo $2$ instead of modulo $3$, and which is much smaller for $d\geq 13$. The remaining primes are dealt with using results of Derickx and Stoll and extensive computations with the modular curves $X_1(p)$ over $\F_2$ for $p\leq 163$.
\end{abstract}
