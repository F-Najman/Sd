## Verdict

I found no gap that affects the results. The arguments are of four kinds (global bound, Derickx-Stoll criteria,
the Hecke obstruction of DKSS Lemma 8.6 at 2, and a small number of special arguments), and each of them is correct
as stated, assuming the certificates are what the ledgers say. The determinations are

    S(9) = S(11) = Primes(31);  S(10) = S(13) = S(16) = Primes(37);  S(12) = S(15) = Primes(37) + {43};
    S(14) = S(17) = S(18) = S(19) = Primes(43);  S(20) = Primes(47) + {61};  S(21) = Primes(47);
    S(22) = Primes(53) + {67}.

## External inputs that I checked

1. Raynaud's theorem, Abbes-Ullmo, Compositio 103 (1996), Appendix A, Theorem A.1: for an exact sequence of abelian
   varieties over a dvr with ramification index e = p - 1 (here p = 2, e = 1) and semi-abelian middle term, the
   sequence of tangent spaces of the Neron models is exact up to a cokernel (Z/p)^r. Checked in the PDF (numdam) and
   in Agashe-Ribet-Stein, Section 4.4. This gives 2M ⊆ L in the dyadic bound (paper, Prop. 4.4 a)).
2. Abbes-Ullmo, Lemma 2.1: the q-expansion pairing between T and the integral cusp forms is perfect. Checked (PDF).
3. Booker-Lee-Strombergsson (JLMS 2020): Selberg's eigenvalue conjecture holds for Gamma_1(N), N <= 880. Checked
   (abstract). Gives gon_Q X_1(p) >= (p^2-1)/192 for p <= 880, used at 97 and 127.
4. Varivoda, *Gonality and quintic points on intermediate modular curves* (preprint, hexagonmath 2610.00219v1),
   Theorem D: gon_Q X_1(43) = 24 (and 41: 22, 47: 29). Checked in `~/refereeing/Varivoda_intermediate`. Used only for
   p = 43, d = 16. The F_2 certificate in `audit11/gonality43*.m` gives gon >= 16 independently.
5. Silverberg's theorem, in the form of Prasad-Yogananda (arXiv math/9810208, Section 1): if E/K has CM by an order
   in k and a point of order N, then phi(N) <= delta mu [K:Q] with delta = 1/2 if k ⊆ K. Checked (PDF).
6. DKSS: Prop. 3.1 (rank-zero primes 41, 47, 59, 71), Lemma 6.7 (intersection formula, needs p > 2dM), Cor. 6.6,
   Prop. 6.3, Cor. 4.3/4.4, Prop. 5.3, Lemma 8.2, Lemma 8.6. Checked in the text.
7. Derickx-Stoll Part I (arXiv 2505.14109v1): Thm 3.3, Prop. 4.1, Remark 4.4, Prop. 5.2/Table 2, Cor. 7.3-7.5.
   Checked in the text.

Not checked (taken from the proofs): the table numbers in Adzaga-Keller-Michaud-Jacobs-Najman-Ozman-Vukorepa
(Table 14 for X_0(113), Table 16 for X_0(127)), Box Section 4.7 for X_0(73), Hasegawa-Shimura/Jeon for X_0(61)
not trigonal over Q (the proofs also check this directly), Parent Prop. 1.9 (only used in Remark 4.5).

## New mathematics in the proofs, checked line by line

1. **The dyadic version of Oesterle's bound** (`dyadic/`, paper Prop. 4.4). If T_1 e, ..., T_{2d} e are independent
   in Te/2Te, then a trace divisor on X_0(p) cannot reduce mod 2 to d times a cusp. Proof: Raynaud gives 2M ⊆ L;
   doubling the divisor puts it in the residue disc mod 4 and kills the reduction-kernel 2-torsion; a map whose
   pullbacks are 2x_i + (quadratic terms) is injective on (4Z_2)^m. Correct. The computational input is
   DKSS Lemma 6.7 with m = 2d, i.e. p > 4dM.
2. **The relative formal immersion lemma** (integral at 2; paper Lemma 2.10). Correct (standard).
3. **The odd-isogeny trick** (paper Lemma 2.7 iii) and Lemma 4.9): if A_3 has odd degree on (h-1)J, then
   (h-1)J(Q) has odd order and a 2-torsion class y in the image is 0. Correct.
4. **p = 61, d = 15** (genus 8 quotient W = X_1(61)/<4> and the involution s). Correct.
5. **Injectivity of torsion reduction on J_1(43)** via the genus 9 quotient. Correct given the computations.

## Simplifications made in the paper

1. **Silverberg's bound replaces all the Frobenius "CM branch" certificates.** A non-cuspidal point of X_1(p) fixed
   by a non-trivial diamond has CM by Z[i] or Z[zeta_3] defined over its field, so its degree is >= (p-1)/2 or
   (p-1)/3 (paper Lemma 2.1, 2.2). For p > 67 and d <= 22 this makes every diamond orbit on a trace divisor free.
   This removes `cm_small.m`, `cm_branch.m` (p = 151, d = 20), the j = 0/1728 branch checks at 97, 109, 113, 127,
   137, the explicit fixed-point computation at 89 (d = 22), Riemann-Hurwitz at 61, and the "real points" argument
   for odd degrees.
2. **d = 9 is proved with the same certificates as d = 10, 11** (`audit11`, full curves, census through degree 11,
   formal immersion padded from degree 11), plus two degree-9 arguments at 37 and 73. The nonic computations
   (`degree09/`, partly on quotients) become an independent second check. At 37 the degree 6 orbit is excluded by
   the stabilizer <8> (order 6) instead of 1140 formal immersions at the sextic lifts.
3. **NC_7 at 43** is excluded in all degrees by one certificate, (<3>-1)A_3 C not principal (`degree13/cusp43.m`),
   instead of the stabilizer argument with the 2-torsion computation for d <= 11.
4. **SS_7 at 113 and SS_12 at 109** are excluded in all degrees by the Hecke certificates computed in degree 21.
5. **p = 53, d = 14, 15, 16** use the single bound gon_F2 X_1(53) >= 20 (degree 16 computation).
6. **Prop. 4.1 data**: p = 109 and 113 use (a, l, n) = (2, 2, 5) also in degree 9 (the nonic paper used (6,2,6) and
   (3,3,7)); the orbit lengths then follow from Lemma 2.2.

## Points for the author

1. **Overlap with Derickx-Stoll [DSt9].** The merged degree 6-9 paper credits S(9) to an unpublished preprint of
   Derickx and Stoll (Part II?). This paper reproves S(9) and goes to d = 22. The wording in the introduction says
   that they "also determine S(9)"; check this and the title of [DSt9].
2. **gon_Q X_1(43) in the nonic/merged paper.** The nonic paper cites "gon_Q X_1(43) >= 17 [DvH, Section 5]"; the
   degree 11 audit says that the published bound in DvH is only >= 13 and that the stronger value is from the website
   of DvH. Worth checking in the merged paper (Varivoda's Theorem D now gives 24).
3. **The level 97 correction.** The first S(15) and S(16) computations used alpha = (s^2-1)(s^4-1) at 97, which does
   not kill the strange characters of order 12. It was replaced by s^12 - 1 and (s-1)Phi_12(s) and everything was
   recomputed (`proofs/S97_correction.txt`). The paper says this in Section 7. The degree 11 and 12 computations
   used (s-1)Phi_12(s), which is correct.
4. **Independent checks.** d <= 11 had an independent audit (`proofs/S10_S11_independent_audit.txt`); d = 13-22 have
   only the automated independent rechecks (Sage ranks, FLINT Pade certificates, independent coverage and census
   counts). No human or separate agent audit of d = 13-22 is claimed anywhere.
5. **Certificates.** The full data (about 363 GB) are only on Mordell

6. **Preprint inputs.** Derickx-Stoll Part I (arXiv), De Leo's thesis via DSt Remark 4.4 (rational 2-primary torsion
