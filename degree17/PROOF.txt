S(17): EXACT DEGREE AND RELATIVE FORMAL IMMERSIONS
29 October 2026

Theorem. For elliptic curves over number fields of exact degree17,
                    S(17)=Primes(43).
The complete prime and support coverage is verified in final_coverage.json.
Failed exploratory calculations are not used as exclusions.

1. OCCURRENCE AND REDUCTION TO EXACT DEGREE

Mazur's examples supply the primes through 7 after extension of scalars
to any degree-17 field. For p=11,13,17,19 the genera of X_1(p) are
1,2,5,7. A rational cusp c and Riemann--Roch give a rational function
with its only pole of exact order 17 at c, since 17>=2g-1. Hilbert
irreducibility gives noncuspidal closed points of exact degree 17.

Explicit examples for p=23,29,31,37,41,43 are retained in occurrences.json,
occurrence23.json and occurrences41_43.json. The three verification
scripts check irreducibility of each field polynomial of degree 17,
nonsingularity, and the exact prime order of (0,0) on the Tate curve
 E=[1-c,-b,-b,0,0],
where
 r=(x^2*y-x*y+y-1)/(x^2*y-x), s=(x*y-y+1)/(x*y),
 b=r*s*(r-1), c=s*(r-1).
The examples at37,41,43 are from the local van Hoeij place table; the
examples at23,29,31 are verified specializations. The equations and their verification, rather than completeness
of the place table, are what is used.

If p>43 belongs to S(17), the associated noncuspidal point of X_1(p)
has degree dividing 17. Its degree cannot be 1 by Mazur. Hence its
rational trace D is irreducible of degree exactly 17.

2. THE CORRECTED DYADIC BOUND

Use the previously established dyadic winding-quotient theorem, with
its integral lattice statement 2M subset L subset M. Equality of the
cotangent lattices is not assumed. Take m=34=2*17 and auxiliary modulus
181. For every u=1,...,180 the 34-by-180 matrix with entries
 epsilon(na)-epsilon(nu/a), 1<=n<=34, 1<=a<=180,
has rank 34 over F_2; epsilon indicates the upper half of nonzero
residues modulo 181. prepare_bound.py retains the matrices, independently
rebuilt and checked in verify_auxiliary.py. The intersection formula is
valid for p>2*34*181=12308.

Thus the doubled-divisor, modulo-4 formal-neighborhood argument of the
earlier dyadic theorem rules out a degree-17 trace supported at a single
rational cusp of X_0(p), for p>12308. The doubling is necessary to retain
validity with the possible factor-2 cotangent-lattice defect. Its proof
uses that torsion in the kernel of reduction over Z_2 is killed by 2.

We improve the naive Hasse cutoff in degree 17. For p>66049, good
reduction with residue degree <=16 is excluded by Hasse. If the residue
degree is 17, an ordinary elliptic curve has a rational 2-torsion point,
so its order is even. Hasse gives order <=131797, whereas an order
divisible by such an odd p would be at least 2p>132098. For supersingular
reduction, j=0 and the three twist orders over F_(2^17) are
 130561=137*953, 131073=3*43691, 131585=5*26317.
bound_ss.m checks all twists and these orders. No prime >66049 divides
them. Thus no place above 2 can give good reduction for p>66049.

Additive reduction cannot support such prime torsion. If the torsion
point lies in the identity component at a multiplicative place, then
p divides 2^f-1 or 2^f+1. Such a place must have f=17, and is therefore
the only place above 2, of ramification index 1. Consequently the trace
on X_0(p) reduces entirely to one cusp. If there is no such place, the
point maps nontrivially into the component group at every place, so
again all conjugates reduce to the same cusp of X_0(p). After applying
Fricke if necessary, this is 17*infinity. The dyadic argument excludes
it. Since 12308<66049, we obtain p<=66049.

5. AN INTEGRAL RELATIVE FORMAL-IMMERSION LEMMA

Let S be smooth of relative dimension d over Z_2, and let L subset S
be a smooth closed formal subspace of relative dimension r through a
special-fiber point x. Let Phi:S->A be a morphism to a smooth group
scheme, vanishing on L. If its differential has rank d-r at x and its
kernel is the tangent space of L, then the completed zero fiber of Phi
at x equals L.

Indeed choose formal coordinates u_1,...,u_r,v_1,...,v_(d-r) with L given
by v=0. Choose d-r target coordinate functions with independent normal
differentials. Since these functions vanish on L they are an invertible
matrix over the complete local ring times v. Their common zero locus
is therefore precisely L. This argument works integrally at2; it uses
no p-adic integration denominator bound and no gonality inequality.

Here Phi is an actual integral Jacobian endomorphism composed with the
Abel map. For a tame diamond subgroup <h> with quotient pi:X->Y, use
odd-prime torsion annihilators composed with h-1. Their image on J(Q)
is zero because (h-1)J(Q) is finite. They also kill pi^*J_Y. Consequently
Phi vanishes along each family F+pi^*Y^(r) when F is a fixed rational
cuspidal divisor. More generally, a fixed local h-invariant divisor
can be included: the remaining contribution is a rational cuspidal
difference, killed by the odd-prime annihilator.

The exported cotangent matrices use the actual integral maps after
Fricke transport. In the q-expansion basis these are, modulo2,
 (D_h-1)*(T_q-D_q-q),
up to invertible diamond factors. Both support and cusp columns use
the same map and basis. At a closed point the coefficient-coordinate
matrix differs from the trace-pairing matrix by an invertible field
basis change, so the ranks are the intrinsic cotangent ranks.

6. RELATIVE EXCLUSIONS AT53,61,73

At53 the only remaining supersingular divisor has degree13. The
subgroup generated by diamond4 has order13 and acts freely on its
geometric support. The divisor is a pullback of a rational point on
Y=X_1(53)/<diamond4>. With any degree4 rational-cusp complement F,
L=F+pi^*Y has dimension1. The maps for q=3,5 have transverse rank16
in all 23751 cusp configurations. Thus any rational D in the disk
belongs to this pullback family and contains F, contradicting its
irreducibility.

At61 the supersingular divisor has degree15. Diamond3 has order5 and
acts freely, so it is a pullback of a degree3 divisor on the quotient.
For every degree2 rational-cusp complement F, L=F+pi^*Y^(3) has
relative dimension3. The transverse rank is14 in all465 configurations.
Again D would contain F and cannot be irreducible.

At73 the difficult case is the supersingular degree6 divisor Z with
a rational-cusp complement C of degree11. Put h=diamond2 of order9.
Its order3 subgroup fixes the geometric points in Z. The stabilizer
and nontrivial order3 tangent action give unique lifts of these points
in the tame fixed locus over unramified extensions of Z_2. Their trace
Z0 is an h-invariant local divisor of degree6. This is also checked
by the rank6 transverse calculation at Z.

The 36 rational cusps form four h-orbits of size9. Write
 C=R+pi^*E by extracting the minimum multiplicity on each orbit.
Then r=deg(E) is 0 or1, deg(R)=11-9r>0, and
                     L=Z0+R+pi^*Y^(r)
is a smooth local family of dimension r on which Phi vanishes.
The four supersingular translates and every normalized cusp pattern
are checked by relative_prefixes.cpp. There are760178194 patterns
for each translate, independently counted by dynamic programming.
The kernel dimension is exactly r: the only relations are the full
h-fiber directions. The integral relative lemma forces D into L.
Its fixed rational cusp contribution R is nonzero, a contradiction.

The other pure supersingular/nonrational-cusp cases at73 have support
degrees12,9,15. The existing actual-Jacobian certificate shows diamond2
acts trivially on rational2-torsion. Thus the torsion class
 y=[h(D-C)-(D-C)] reducing to0 is0, by its norm relation and odd order9.
The gonality bound exceeds17+c for c=5,8,2. Hence hD=D. Its possible
noncuspidal orbit lengths are9 or3, neither compatible with17.

7. RELATIVE EXCLUSION AT103

The cusp test is performed on Y=X_1(103)/H with [Delta:H]=17. Every
normalized degree17 rational-cusp pattern passes except the sum of
all17 rational cusps, each with multiplicity1. Independent enumeration
of the relation space proves that this is the sole deficient pattern.
It is exactly the pullback of the rational cusp on X_0(103).

The additional map (diamond5-1)*(T_3-3-diamond3), on the integral
Jacobian of Y, kills J_Y(Q) since103 is non-strange, and kills the
pullback of J_0(103). Its cotangent rank at this fiber is16. The
relative lemma therefore forces any rational divisor in this disk
to be a pullback of a rational point of X_0(103). Rationality follows
also by uniqueness of the pulled-back divisor and Galois descent.
Mazur rules out the required noncuspidal rational point.

A degree17 point upstairs maps to a point of degree1 or17 on Y. The
first alternative already gives a rational point on X_0(103); the
second is the irreducible divisor just considered. This handles the
exceptional cusp disk without any gonality assumption on Y.

8. THE OTHER LOCAL EXCEPTIONS

For any h fixing the nonrational reduction and C the rational cusp
lift, finite image of h-1 implies y=[h(D-C)-(D-C)] is torsion reducing
to0, hence2y=0. If y=0 and gon>17+c, or simply gon>2*(17+c), then
h(D-C)=D-C as divisors. Disjoint cuspidal and noncuspidal support gives
hD=D. The possible noncuspidal orbit lengths are ord(h), ord(h)/2 at
j=1728, or ord(h)/3 at j=0. Irreducibility of D rules out any of these
lengths except1 or17.

At89 the nonrational cusp has degree11, with complement6. The odd-
degree Hecke-isogeny certificate on the h=diamond2 anti-Jacobian,
proved on both Betti signs in degree16, gives y=0. Indeed N_h*y=0
and 2y=0 imply y=(11-N_h)*y in (h-1)J, where the odd-degree
annihilator is injective on2-torsion. The gonality bound
exceeds23, and h has order11, so invariance is impossible.

At97 the supersingular degree16 divisor is fixed by the order4 subgroup.
The doubled bound 2*(17+1)=36 is below the gonality lower bound. Thus
D is invariant; the possible orbit lengths4 and2 do not divide17.

At109 the supersingular degrees are9 and12, fixed by diamond23 of
order18. The doubled bounds50 and44 are below the gonality bound.
The possible orbit lengths18,9,6 are incompatible with17.

At113, the degree7 supersingular point, two such points, and the degree14
nonrational cusp are fixed by diamond2 of order14. The doubled bounds
54 and40 are below the gonality bound. The exceptional ordinary degree8
orbit (and its pair exceptions) is fixed by diamond40 of order8; the
largest doubled bound is52. None of these orbit lengths is1 or17.
In the mixed degree7+8 case diamond98 is an involution fixing the whole
reduction. The doubled bound38 is sufficient. Invariance forces j=1728,
excluded through degree17 by cm_small.m using Frobenius at3.

At127 the nonrational cusp has degree7 and all supersingular points
have degree14. These are fixed by diamond2 of order7. The doubled
bound is at most54, below325*(127^2-1)/65536. The ordinary degree9
orbit of size7 is fixed by the order9 subgroup, and its doubled bound
is50. Invariance excludes these cases. The remaining ordinary and
mixed cases, apart from the following partially relative case, are the
retained principal-divisor obstructions.

The mixed degree9 ordinary orbit plus degree7 nonrational cusp and one
rational cusp F is treated using two quotients. Put H9=<diamond(3^7)>
and H7=<diamond2>, of orders9 and7. The ordinary divisor is a pullback
of a rational point on X/H9, and the nonrational cusp divisor is a
pullback of a rational point on X/H7. Both actions are free on the
corresponding geometric support. Use
 alpha=(diamond(3^7)-1)*(diamond2-1)
composed with torsion annihilators at2 and3. At2, the needed hypothesis
holds because rational torsion on J_1(127) is cuspidal, as recorded in
Derickx--Stoll Remark4.4. These are actual integral maps killing J(Q)
and both pulled-back Jacobians. They vanish on
             L=F+pi9^*(X/H9)+pi7^*(X/H7),
a smooth local family of dimension2. There is one relative position of
the two closed orbits under Delta, since their orbit sizes7 and9 are
coprime. All63 positions of F have transverse rank15. The integral
relative lemma forces D into L, contradicting the fixed cusp F.
verify_relative127.py independently reconstructs every cotangent entry,
including the nonrational-cusp coordinates obtained from the nodal
Tate curve with parameter a primitive127th root in F_(2^7).

At97 the operator sigma^12-1 is intentional. The inherited expression
