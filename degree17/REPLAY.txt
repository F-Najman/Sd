S(17) proof replay

Run with Magma V2.30-1 and Sage Python, from the workspace root.
The installed mdmagma library is at the AttachSpec paths in the scripts.
Authoritative status: final_coverage.json. Mathematical implications:
PROOF.txt. The supplied model data and lower-degree pair certificates
are intentional dependencies, not completeness assertions about a
sporadic-point database.

all completion markers and source metadata have passed. The point
coordinates at97 are reused from the older models, but the ordinary

asserts that enough coefficients were actually retained.
